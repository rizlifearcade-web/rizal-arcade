# Game 07 — Global Sojourn

## Approved topic boundary

Rizal's travels, intellectual networks, publications abroad, medical training, and reform work. Do not turn this into a general world-history or geography game.

## Contributor assignment

- Build the mechanic only in this folder.
- Use `content.ts` for the sourced challenge bank.
- Use local or clearly licensed visual and audio assets.
- Keep touch targets large and text readable on 1024×768 and 834×1194 screens.
- Add a pre-game instruction entry when the project integrator registers the game.
- Do not add it to `registry.tsx` yourself; integration happens after review.

## Acceptance checklist

- At least 50 non-repetitive, sourced challenges.
- One clear mechanic that is not ordinary multiple choice.
- Keyboard, touch, and screen-reader labels.
- Score, lives or attempts, results, audio toggle, and replay.
- No horizontal scrolling at the supported tablet sizes.
- Tests for content completeness and duplicate IDs.
